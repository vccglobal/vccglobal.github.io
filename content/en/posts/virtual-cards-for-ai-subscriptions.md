Signing up for ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro or a Midjourney plan is supposed to take two minutes. For a lot of people outside the US and EU, it takes considerably longer — the card is entered, the page spins, and the charge comes back declined.

The reason is rarely a lack of funds. Most AI platforms bill through US payment processors that apply issuing-country rules, and many local debit cards are simply not enabled for recurring international charges.

## What actually goes wrong

Three failure modes account for most declines:

- **The card is not enabled for cross-border recurring billing.** A one-off purchase may clear while a monthly subscription is refused.
- **The billing address does not match the issuer's records.** US processors run an address verification check, and a mismatch is treated as a risk signal.
- **The currency is unsupported.** Some issuers block USD-denominated recurring charges outright.

## Using a virtual card instead

A virtual credit card is issued in a supported region, funded on demand, and used only for the subscription in question. Because the card is created for this purpose, the country, currency and billing profile line up with what the merchant expects.

Practical advantages for subscription spend:

- **One card per service.** Cancelling a subscription becomes a matter of freezing the card, with no risk of a forgotten renewal.
- **Predictable currency.** Charges settle in the card's own currency, so the amount on the statement matches the price on the pricing page.
- **A clear spending record.** Each service has its own transaction history rather than a single mixed statement.

## Beyond AI tools

The same setup covers advertising accounts on Facebook, Google and TikTok, streaming subscriptions such as YouTube Premium and Netflix, developer services like cloud hosting and API credits, and one-off purchases from overseas merchants.

For teams running paid advertising, the per-card separation matters more than for individuals: a card can be issued per campaign or per client, which makes reconciliation at the end of the month straightforward.
