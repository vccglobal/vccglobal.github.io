# Step 2: Add a Virtual Card to Your PayPal Wallet

The process of adding a virtual Visa or Mastercard is similar to adding a normal bank card.

### On PayPal Website:

1. Log in to your PayPal account
2. Open **Wallet**
3. Select **Link a card**
4. Enter your virtual card details
5. Confirm the card information
6. Complete any verification steps if required

PayPal’s official process allows users to add cards by entering card details and verifying the payment method. (PayPal)

### On PayPal Mobile App:

1. Open the PayPal app
2. Go to account settings or wallet section
3. Choose adding a new card
4. Enter Visa/Mastercard virtual card information
5. Save the payment method

After successful linking, the card can appear as an available payment option.

# Common Problems When Linking a Virtual Card to PayPal

Although many users successfully connect virtual cards, payment issues can happen because of account settings, merchant rules, or card restrictions.

## 1. Card Cannot Be Added

Possible reasons:

- Incorrect card information
- Unsupported card type
- Billing details do not match
- Card has not been activated

Solution:

- Check card details carefully
- Confirm the card status
- Make sure the card supports online international payments

---

## 2. PayPal Payment Is Declined

Possible causes:

- Insufficient balance
- Merchant does not accept certain cards
- Security verification triggered
- Transaction location mismatch

Suggestions:

- Maintain enough balance before payment
- Avoid repeated failed attempts
- Complete PayPal account verification when required

---

## 3. Subscription Payments Fail Later

Some services use recurring billing. A card that works for a one-time payment may still require additional support for automatic charges.

Before using a virtual card for subscriptions:

- Check whether recurring payments are supported
- Keep enough balance available
- Monitor expiration dates
