For many international travelers visiting China for the first time, one of the biggest questions is not where to go — it is how to pay.

China has developed a highly digital payment environment. In many cities, daily activities such as shopping at malls, ordering food at restaurants, taking the subway, paying for taxis, and buying small items are commonly completed through mobile payment apps.

For foreign visitors, understanding how to use Alipay with an overseas Visa or Mastercard can make traveling in China much easier. Instead of relying only on cash or searching for places that accept international cards, travelers can prepare a mobile payment method before arriving.

This article explains how overseas visitors can use Alipay in China, connect foreign bank cards, and handle everyday payments such as shopping, dining, transportation, and other daily expenses.

---

# How Do Foreign Visitors Pay in China?

Unlike many countries where cash and physical bank cards are still commonly used, mobile payments are deeply integrated into everyday life in China.

Common payment situations include:

- Shopping malls and retail stores;
- Restaurants and cafes;
- Subway and public transportation;
- Taxi services;
- Convenience stores;
- Tourist attractions.

For many small purchases, QR code payments are one of the most common payment methods.

---

# Paying at Shopping Malls and Stores in China

When visiting Chinese shopping centers, supermarkets, and retail stores, foreign travelers may notice that many payments are completed by scanning QR codes.

Typical payment process:

1. Open Alipay;
2. Scan the merchant QR code or payment code;
3. Confirm the amount;
4. Complete the payment.

This payment method is widely used for:

- Clothing stores;
- Electronics shops;
- Convenience stores;
- Supermarkets;
- Tourist shops.

For travelers who are used to paying with physical cards, preparing Alipay before arriving can make daily shopping much more convenient.

---

# Paying at Restaurants in China with Alipay

Restaurants in China often use digital ordering and payment systems.

Visitors may encounter:

- QR codes placed on tables;
- Self-service ordering systems;
- Mobile payment counters.

Common situations:

### Ordering Food

Some restaurants allow customers to scan a QR code, view the menu, order directly, and pay through mobile payment.

### Paying the Bill

After finishing a meal, customers can usually complete payment through Alipay instead of waiting for traditional card processing.

For foreign visitors, connecting an overseas Visa or Mastercard to Alipay can reduce the difficulty of making everyday payments.

# Using Alipay for Subway and Transportation

Public transportation is another important payment scenario.

Depending on the city and available services, visitors may use mobile payment options for:

- Subway rides;
- Bus transportation;
- Taxi payments;
- Ride-hailing services.

Large cities such as Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen have extensive digital payment systems.

Before traveling, it is recommended to check whether the transportation service in your destination supports international users and overseas payment methods.

# How to Link an Overseas Visa or Mastercard to Alipay

Foreign visitors can prepare Alipay by adding an eligible overseas bank card.

Before setup, prepare:

- Visa or Mastercard card;
- Passport information if required;
- International phone number;
- Basic account information.

General steps:

## Step 1: Create an Alipay Account

Download the official Alipay application and register an account.

Complete the required information according to the platform instructions.

---

## Step 2: Add a Foreign Bank Card

Go to the payment or wallet section and select the option to add a card.

Enter:

- Card number;
- Expiration date;
- Security code;
- Required verification information.

Depending on your card issuer and region, additional verification may be required.

---

## Step 3: Complete Account Verification

Some payment features may require identity verification.

Foreign visitors should follow the official verification process and provide accurate information.

# Can Foreigners Use Alipay Without a Chinese Bank Account?

Many travelers assume they need a Chinese bank account before using mobile payments in China.

In many cases, overseas visitors can access supported Alipay payment functions by connecting eligible international cards.

However, available features may depend on:

- Country or region;
- Card issuer;
- Account verification status;
- Current Alipay service availability.

Setting up payment before arriving in China can help avoid problems during travel.

# Common Problems When Using Foreign Cards with Alipay

## Overseas Card Cannot Be Added

Possible reasons:

- Card type is not supported;
- Incorrect card information;
- Verification not completed;
- Regional limitations.

Suggestions:

- Check card details carefully;
- Confirm international payment availability;
- Complete all required verification steps.

---

## Payment Is Declined

Possible causes:

- Insufficient balance;
- Security verification;
- Merchant restrictions;
- Card payment limitations.

Recommendations:

- Keep enough available balance;
- Confirm your card supports international transactions;
- Avoid repeated failed payment attempts.

---

## QR Payment Does Not Work

Possible reasons:

- Account setup is incomplete;
- Payment function is unavailable;
- Merchant system limitations.

Checking account settings and payment status can help identify the issue.
