# How to Add a Virtual Visa/Mastercard Card to Apple Wallet?

The setup process is generally straightforward.

## Step 1: Open Apple Wallet

On your iPhone:

1. Open the Wallet app;
2. Tap the “+” button;
3. Select the option to add a payment card.

---

## Step 2: Enter Virtual Card Information

Provide the requested details:

- Card number;
- Expiration date;
- CVV code;
- Additional verification information.

Depending on the issuer, you may need to complete an extra verification step.

---

## Step 3: Complete Verification

After submitting the card details, Apple Pay and the card issuer may verify the information.

Once approved, the card will appear inside Apple Wallet and can be selected for supported payments.

# How to Use Apple Pay for Offline Purchases?

If the card supports Apple Pay and the merchant accepts contactless payments, users can pay at physical locations.

The process:

1. Hold the iPhone or Apple Watch near the payment terminal;
2. Open Apple Pay;
3. Select the card;
4. Complete identity verification;
5. Wait for payment confirmation.

Common places where contactless payments may be available:

- Shopping malls;
- Restaurants;
- Supermarkets;
- Convenience stores;
- Travel locations.

# Common Problems When Adding Virtual Cards to Apple Pay

## The Virtual Card Cannot Be Added

Possible reasons:

- The issuer does not support Apple Pay;
- The card type is not eligible;
- Regional restrictions;
- Incorrect card information.

Possible solutions:

- Confirm Apple Pay compatibility;
- Check card status;
- Review entered information.

---

## Subscription Payment Fails

Possible causes:

- Insufficient balance;
- Payment restrictions;
- Verification issues;
- Service does not support the selected payment method.

Suggestions:

- Maintain enough balance;
- Confirm subscription requirements;
- Check card availability before renewal dates.

---

## Contactless Payment Does Not Work

Possible reasons:

- Merchant does not support Apple Pay;
- Terminal does not support NFC payments;
- Card verification is incomplete.

Checking merchant support and card settings can help identify the issue.
